Clay Hero Privacy Notice

English overview. Read the full German privacy notice for complete details.

Controller and scope

René Ingerl / Remedion Games, Josef-Huber-Straße 4b/1/7, 2620 Ternitz, Austria. Email remediongames@gmail.com. This notice concerns Clay Hero for Android and this information page.

Local game data and settings

The test build does not require an account. The app keeps progress, chapter completion, arena records, equipment, currency, unlocks, shop information, and settings on your device. The current shop uses in-game silver only. There is no online leaderboard or active Google Play Games integration, and no automatic upload of your save.

Diagnostics and optional feedback

Technical logs are stored on your device and are not uploaded automatically. “Send feedback” opens an editable email draft with selected technical and game information. You can edit it or discard it. The draft is sent only if you submit it in your email app.

Email contact

We use your email address and message to answer your inquiry. Our inbox is provided by Google/Gmail.

Website and external links

The in-game links open in your browser after you select them. GitHub Pages processes technical access data for hosting and security. This privacy page does not load advertising or analytics or send your saved game to the website.

Google Play and Android backups

Google processes data when you use Google Play to download or update the app. See the German notice for details about distribution and Android backups.

Advertising and consent

The test build has no active ads. The ad interface is a placeholder. Planned advertising and consent for the first store release are described in the German notice.

Google Play Billing and Unity IAP

Real-money purchases are not active in the test build. Google Play Billing and Unity IAP are planned for the first store release.

Other potential services

There is no Clay Hero account, cloud save, usage analytics, or cloud diagnostics in the current test build.

Recipients and transfers

See the German notice for details about third-party services, recipients, and international transfers.

Retention and deletion

Local data and voluntarily sent messages are handled differently. See the German notice for retention and deletion details.

Your rights and choices

Contact Remedion Games by email to exercise applicable privacy rights. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Changes to this notice

See the full German notice for the current version and detailed terms.
