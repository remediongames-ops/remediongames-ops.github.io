Remedion Games

A small studio with its own style.

Remedion Games is the independent games project of René Ingerl, based in Ternitz, Austria. The studio makes mobile games, characters, and shorts with immediate gameplay, offbeat humor, and a distinctive look. Its current focus is Clay Hero, a clay adventure for Android. How FrogSlap began

FrogSlap started with a simple idea: a frog jumps onto a lily pad and the player has to react at the right moment. That grew into a game with varied enemies, ten worlds, bosses, runes, powers, rewards, and a dedicated Chaos Mode.

Much of development goes into refining how hits feel, making menus readable, cleaning up audio and animation, improving performance, and making the game easy to understand from the first few seconds.

The game and the cartoons

The FrogSlap cartoons tell stories beyond the game’s runs. Familiar enemies have roles of their own. The four-armed Mutant travels through contaminated areas toward the lab with Plague, Bomb Frog, and their companions.

The cartoons tell stories that stand on their own and give players more of the world to explore.
