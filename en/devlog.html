Behind the scenes

Polishing FrogSlap.

A game keeps evolving after launch. Read about the decisions, problems, and improvements behind FrogSlap.

September 8, 2026 · Development

From prototype to a proper slap

The current polish pass focuses on making every hit feel physical with particles, brief screen movement, clearer sounds, and better animations. The HUD is also being refined so hearts, points, and other key indicators remain readable on small screens.

Too many particles can obscure enemies and hurt performance, so effects are prioritized, batched, and tested on lower-end Android devices.

September 6, 2026 · Game systems

Why Endless Mode became Chaos Mode

Repeating runs forever gives higher numbers but few surprises. Chaos Mode adds its own map, a Chaos meter, and curses that trigger automatically. Each Chaos level changes the environment and makes the run more dangerous.

Chaos Mode has distinct rewards including Chaos Shards, relics, profile frames, animated slap skins, and badges earned through play.

September 3, 2026 · Audio and devices

Why the same sound differs from phone to phone

Tablet speakers reproduce slaps, music, and interface sounds differently from headphones or small phone speakers. The mix is being rebalanced across devices so effects stay clear and undistorted and music leaves room for each hit.

August 30, 2026 · Cartoons

Turning enemies into characters

The FrogSlap cartoons take enemies from the game and send them on a journey. Mutant, Plague, the gray flying frog, and Bomb Frog head through the toxic zone toward the lab. The short episodes make room for slapstick and reveal a bit of the world along the way.
