Official game guide

Survive longer. Slap harder.

FrogSlap looks simple: frog appears, finger lands, problem solved. Later worlds punish blind tapping. Here are the essentials, with the surprises left intact.

Run basics

You start with three hearts. Ordinary frogs disappear after one slap, while special enemies change the pace, damage, or behavior of nearby lily pads. Hits fill the boss meter, and combos fill it faster. A full meter starts the world boss fight.

Recognize the enemy’s color and ability first. Rushing to slap the wrong frog can cost more than taking a moment to react.

Hearts and power-ups

Heart

Restores lost health. Use it if the next hit could end your run.

Shield

Blocks the next hit. Especially useful before fast enemy waves and boss phases.

Lightning

Briefly controls multiple targets. Use it when several lily pads fill at once.

Dragonfly

Distracts frogs and buys you time. It does not deal damage.

Dangerous enemies

Orange frogs move faster, red frogs deal double damage, and purple foes bring reinforcements. Shamans summon more frogs, shield enemies protect their neighbors, and Plague can poison you. Bomb Frog needs precise timing. RobFrog later joins in, blocking adjacent pads and sending out a shockwave.

Watch nearby pads as well as your target. Many abilities affect other pads. As the screen gets busier, the order of your slaps matters more.

Runes and lasting progression

Runes let you change your build between runs. You start with three slots and unlock more as you progress. Duplicate runes become essences used to upgrade existing runes. Choose abilities that work together to create a strong build.

Pick a direction: better defense, faster boss progress, or more aggressive powers. Trying to upgrade everything often leaves you with a build that excels at nothing.

Bosses and Chaos Mode

Regular frogs do not appear during boss fights. Watch attack patterns and save defensive powers for dangerous phases. Complete the ten standard worlds to unlock Chaos Mode, where a separate meter fills, curses change your run, and each Chaos level makes the environment more dangerous.

Five tips for better runs

  1. Learn the colors and abilities before trying to tap faster.
  2. Use shields and healing before risky waves. After you die, they’re about as useful as an umbrella in a volcano.
  3. Prioritize enemies that block other pads or call reinforcements.
  4. Build your rune setup around a clear goal and upgrade runes that work well together.
  5. Chase combos only when you can still tell the enemies apart.
